# Extension — Legal / Administrative Procedure

Typical workstreams:
- eligibility
- required documents
- issuing authority
- document validity/expiry
- translation/certification
- application packet
- appointment/submission
- additional requests
- result / post-result actions

Typical controls:
- required-document matrix
- issuer / acquisition route
- required-by date
- validity window
- submission manifest
- receipt confirmation
- additional-request loop

Official authority guidance is the primary source.
Do not infer required documents or deadlines when the authority source is missing or ambiguous.
